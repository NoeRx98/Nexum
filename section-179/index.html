Questions

What people ask before they apply.

What are the minimum requirements?

We typically work with established businesses operating for at least a year with consistent monthly revenue. This program isn't designed for brand-new startups with no revenue history, or for personal loans.

Will checking my options hurt my credit?

No. Checking your options won't affect your credit. If you move forward with a specific lender, they may run a full credit check as part of underwriting.

Do I have to put money down?

Not always. Because the equipment itself secures the financing, no-money-down and low-down structures are common for qualified borrowers. We'll show you the options side by side so you can protect your cash flow.

Is this a loan or a lease?

We offer a range of products, including traditional loans and equipment financing agreements (EFAs). We'll help you choose the structure that best fits your tax and cash-flow needs.

What equipment do you finance?

Commercial equipment across construction, transportation, agriculture and manufacturing, new or used: excavators, dozers, loaders, trucks, trailers and specialized machinery.
