Questions?

We have answers.

What kind of equipment do you finance?

Commercial equipment across construction, transportation, agriculture, and manufacturing — from excavators and dozers to commercial trucks, trailers, and specialized machinery. We also offer business lines of credit for working capital needs.

What are the minimum requirements?

We typically work with established businesses operating for at least a year with consistent monthly revenue. The best way to know for sure is our quick application — checking your options won't affect your credit. If you move forward with a specific lender, they may run a full credit check as part of underwriting.

How long does the process take?

Speed is the point. Many clients receive a decision within 24–48 hours and can be funded within a few business days after documents are submitted.

Is this a loan or a lease?

We offer a range of products, including traditional loans and equipment financing agreements (EFAs). We'll help you choose the structure that best fits your tax and cash-flow needs.

Do I have to put money down?

Not always. Because the equipment itself secures the financing, no-money-down and low-down structures are common for qualified borrowers. We'll show you the options side by side so you can protect your cash flow.

Does Nexum serve businesses outside of Texas?

Yes. Nexum is based in the Dallas-Fort Worth area and works with established businesses across the United States, not just in Texas.
